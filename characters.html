★ Sonic's Friends ★

Sonic the Hedgehog

Species: Hedgehog

Height: 100 cm

Weight: 35 kg

Special Ability: Supersonic speed

Sonic is a free-spirited adventurer who loves exploring new places and helping others. His incredible speed allows him to overcome obstacles and challenge powerful enemies.

Although he enjoys joking around, Sonic takes protecting his friends seriously. He believes everyone deserves freedom and refuses to let villains control the world.

Miles "Tails" Prower

Species: Fox

Height: 80 cm

Weight: 20 kg

Special Abilities: Flight and engineering

Tails is Sonic's best friend and a gifted young inventor. By spinning his two tails, he can fly through the air and reach difficult places.

His intelligence makes him an important teammate. Whether repairing machines or piloting aircraft, Tails always tries to support his friends.

Knuckles the Echidna

Species: Echidna

Height: 110 cm

Weight: 40 kg

Special Abilities: Strength, gliding and climbing

Knuckles protects the Master Emerald on Angel Island. His powerful fists and climbing abilities make him a formidable fighter and treasure hunter.

He can be stubborn and overly trusting, but his loyalty and sense of responsibility make him a dependable ally.

Amy Rose

Species: Hedgehog

Height: 90 cm

Special Ability: Piko Piko Hammer

Amy is an energetic and compassionate adventurer who stands up for the people she cares about. Her signature hammer helps her face dangerous enemies.

Her optimism, courage, and determination often inspire those around her.

Cream the Rabbit

Species: Rabbit

Height: 70 cm

Weight: 12 kg

Special Ability: Flying with her ears

Cream is a polite young rabbit who enjoys helping others. She can fly by moving her large ears and often travels with her Chao companion, Cheese.

Despite her gentle personality, Cream is brave when her friends need help.

Big the Cat

Species: Cat

Height: 200 cm

Weight: 280 kg

Favorite Activity: Fishing

Big is a relaxed and friendly cat who enjoys spending time outdoors, especially fishing with his friend Froggy.

Although he prefers peaceful days, Big is willing to help his friends whenever trouble appears.

★ Team Dark ★

Shadow the Hedgehog

Species: Hedgehog

Height: 100 cm

Weight: 35 kg

Special Abilities: Chaos Control and Chaos Spear

Shadow was created as the Ultimate Life Form. He possesses extraordinary speed and can manipulate Chaos energy to perform powerful techniques.

Independent and determined, Shadow follows his own principles while protecting what matters to him.

Rouge the Bat

Species: Bat

Height: 105 cm

Special Abilities: Flight and powerful kicks

Rouge is a skilled spy and treasure hunter who has a particular interest in valuable gemstones.

Her confidence, intelligence, and ability to gather information make her a valuable member of Team Dark.

E-123 Omega

Height: 150 cm

Special Abilities: Heavy weapons and combat

Omega is a powerful robot originally created by Dr. Eggman. After turning against his creator, he became an important member of Team Dark.

His enormous arsenal and determination make him especially dangerous in battle.

★ Team Chaotix ★

Vector the Crocodile

Species: Crocodile

Height: 180 cm

Special Ability: Investigation

Vector leads the Chaotix Detective Agency. He enjoys solving mysteries and earning money for the team.

Beneath his loud personality, Vector has a generous heart and cares about helping others.

Espio the Chameleon

Species: Chameleon

Height: 110 cm

Special Abilities: Ninjutsu and camouflage

Espio is the calm and disciplined member of Team Chaotix. His ninja training allows him to move quietly and surprise enemies.

His patience and analytical thinking help the team investigate difficult mysteries.

Charmy Bee

Species: Bee

Height: 70 cm

Special Abilities: Flight and stinger attacks

Charmy is an energetic young bee who brings excitement to Team Chaotix. He loves having fun and often acts before thinking.

His enthusiasm and flying abilities make him a useful teammate during adventures.

★ Other Heroes ★

Silver the Hedgehog

Species: Hedgehog

Height: 100 cm

Special Ability: Psychokinesis

Silver is a heroic hedgehog from the future. His psychic abilities allow him to lift and move objects without touching them.

He is determined to create a better future and protect innocent people.

Blaze the Cat

Species: Cat

Height: 95 cm

Special Ability: Pyrokinesis

Blaze is a princess from another dimension and the guardian of the Sol Emeralds.

She can control fire and uses her abilities to defend her world. Although reserved, Blaze values friendship and teamwork.

★ Babylon Rogues ★

Jet the Hawk

Species: Hawk

Height: 100 cm

Specialty: Extreme Gear racing

Jet leads the Babylon Rogues and considers himself one of the greatest Extreme Gear racers.

His competitive personality creates a strong rivalry with Sonic.

Wave the Swallow

Species: Swallow

Height: 110 cm

Specialty: Extreme Gear engineering

Wave is the Babylon Rogues' talented mechanic and strategist. She builds and improves Extreme Gear equipment.

Her technical knowledge helps the team compete against skilled racers.

Storm the Albatross

Species: Albatross

Height: 140 cm

Special Ability: Physical strength

Storm is the strongest member of the Babylon Rogues. He is fiercely loyal to Jet and uses his strength to challenge opponents.

Although easily frustrated, Storm is dedicated to supporting his teammates.

★ Eggman's Empire ★

Dr. Eggman

Species: Human

Height: 185 cm

Specialty: Robotics and engineering

Dr. Eggman is a brilliant scientist determined to conquer the world. He creates enormous machines and armies of robots to carry out his plans.

Despite his repeated defeats, Eggman continues inventing new ways to challenge Sonic.

Metal Sonic

Height: 100 cm

Weight: 125.2 kg

Special Abilities: High-speed flight, Black Shield, and Overdrive

Metal Sonic is a robotic version of Sonic created by Dr. Eggman. Designed to rival Sonic's speed and abilities, Metal Sonic is one of Eggman's most dangerous creations.

His advanced technology allows him to challenge powerful opponents. Although originally programmed to obey Eggman, Metal Sonic has sometimes developed ambitions of his own.

Neo Metal Sonic

Height: 165 cm

Special Abilities: Copying biological data, transformation, and advanced combat

Neo Metal Sonic is an upgraded form of Metal Sonic who appears in Sonic Heroes. He gains the ability to copy information from other characters and use their strengths to increase his own power.

Determined to prove his superiority, Neo Metal Sonic turns against Eggman's authority and becomes a major threat to Sonic and his friends.

Orbot

Height: 87 cm

Weight: 30 kg

Special Abilities: Data analysis and cleaning

Orbot is one of Dr. Eggman's robotic assistants. He helps organize Eggman's operations and works alongside Cubot.

Orbot is usually the more intelligent and responsible member of the pair. His sarcastic comments sometimes irritate Eggman, but he continues carrying out his duties.

Cubot

Height: 90 cm

Weight: 39 kg

Special Feature: Interchangeable voice chips

Cubot is another robotic assistant who works for Dr. Eggman. Unlike Orbot, Cubot is easily distracted and often makes mistakes.

His unusual behavior provides comic relief during Eggman's adventures. Despite his mistakes, Cubot remains loyal to his creator.

Sage

Height: 105 cm

Special Abilities: Artificial intelligence, data analysis, and cyberspace control

Sage is an advanced artificial intelligence created by Dr. Eggman. She plays an important role in Sonic Frontiers.

Sage is highly intelligent and capable of analyzing dangerous situations. Over time, she develops a deeper understanding of emotions and forms a meaningful connection with Eggman.

★ Powerful Enemies ★

Chaos

Species: Mutated Chao

Height: Varies

Special Abilities: Water manipulation and transformation

Chaos is a mysterious water-like creature connected to the ancient history of the Chaos Emeralds. Long ago, Chaos protected the Chao and the Master Emerald's shrine.

After witnessing violence against the Chao, Chaos became consumed by anger. In Sonic Adventure, Chaos absorbs the Chaos Emeralds and transforms into increasingly powerful forms, including Perfect Chaos.

Mephiles the Dark

Origin: A fragment of Solaris

Special Abilities: Darkness manipulation, illusions, and supernatural powers

Mephiles is a mysterious and dangerous enemy who appears in Sonic the Hedgehog (2006). He is connected to Solaris and takes on an appearance resembling Shadow.

Rather than relying only on physical strength, Mephiles manipulates others and uses carefully planned schemes to accomplish his goals.

Infinite

Species: Jackal

Gender: Male

Special Abilities: Phantom Ruby illusions and reality manipulation

Infinite is a masked villain introduced in Sonic Forces. Once the leader of a mercenary group, he eventually joins Dr. Eggman's forces.

Using the Phantom Ruby, Infinite creates frightening illusions and powerful virtual environments. His desire to prove his strength makes him a dangerous opponent.

★ IDW Comic Characters ★

Tangle the Lemur

Species: Ring-tailed lemur

Height: 100 cm

Special Abilities: Prehensile tail, agility, and acrobatics

Tangle is an adventurous and energetic hero introduced in the IDW Sonic the Hedgehog comic series.

She enjoys excitement and never hesitates to help someone in danger. Her unusually flexible tail allows her to swing, grab objects, and fight enemies in creative ways.

Tangle's cheerful personality and loyalty make her a dependable friend.

Whisper the Wolf

Species: Wolf

Height: 100 cm

Special Abilities: Wispon combat, stealth, and precision shooting

Whisper is a quiet and mysterious hero introduced in the IDW Sonic the Hedgehog comic series.

She uses a special weapon called a Variable Wispon and works with Wisps to battle dangerous enemies.

Although she prefers keeping to herself, Whisper cares deeply about her friends. Her friendship with Tangle helps her gradually become more comfortable trusting others.
