Welcome to Sonic Visual Content!

Sonic the Hedgehog has appeared in many different forms of entertainment beyond video games. From animated television shows and anime to theatrical movies and online shorts, Sonic's adventures have reached audiences around the world.

Explore 21 Sonic productions from 1993 through 2026, including television series, films, animated shorts, and an audio mystery adventure.

★ Classic Animation (1993–1996) ★

Sonic the Hedgehog (SatAM)

Year: 1993

Type: Animated Television Series

This Saturday morning cartoon presents a darker version of Sonic's world. Sonic and the Freedom Fighters lead a rebellion against Dr. Robotnik, who has taken control of Mobius. The series combines action, adventure, and an ongoing story about restoring freedom.

Sonic the Hedgehog (OVA)

Year: 1996

Type: Japanese Anime

This two-part Japanese anime follows Sonic and Tails on an adventure in the Land of the Sky. Sonic faces powerful enemies, including Metal Sonic, in a story featuring colorful animation and high-speed action.

Sonic Christmas Blast

Year: 1996

Type: Animated Holiday Special

Sonic Christmas Blast is a holiday adventure where Dr. Robotnik attempts to take over Christmas. After replacing Santa Claus with a robotic duplicate, Robotnik threatens the holiday, leaving Sonic to save the day.

★ Anime & Early Animation (2003–2019) ★

Sonic X

Year: 2003

Type: Anime Television Series

Sonic X follows Sonic and his friends after a Chaos Control accident transports them to Earth. Sonic meets a young boy named Chris Thorndyke and begins a series of adventures while searching for a way home.

Sonic: Night of the Werehog

Year: 2008

Type: CGI Animated Short Film

Inspired by Sonic Unleashed, this animated short follows Sonic and Chip as they explore a mysterious haunted house. Mischievous ghosts attempt to frighten Sonic, leading to humorous encounters involving his Werehog form.

Sonic Mania Adventures

Year: 2018

Type: Animated Web Series

Sonic Mania Adventures is a classic-style animated series inspired by the original Sonic games. Its five main episodes follow Sonic, Tails, Knuckles, and other familiar characters through colorful adventures connected to Sonic Mania.

Team Sonic Racing Overdrive

Year: 2019

Type: Promotional Animated Series

Team Sonic Racing Overdrive is a two-part animated adventure promoting Team Sonic Racing. Sonic and his friends compete in exciting races while demonstrating teamwork, rivalry, and their unique personalities.

Chao in Space

Year: 2019

Type: Animated Short Film

Chao in Space brings the fictional movie posters seen in Sonic Adventure to life. The short follows a sleeping Chao dreaming about an exciting adventure in outer space.

★ Movies & Modern Animation (2020–2023) ★

Sonic the Hedgehog

Year: 2020

Type: Live-Action/CGI Movie

Sonic makes his theatrical movie debut in a story about an alien hedgehog living secretly in Green Hills, Montana. After attracting the attention of Dr. Robotnik, Sonic teams up with Tom Wachowski to protect his new home.

Sonic Colors: Rise of the Wisps

Year: 2021

Type: Animated Web Series

This two-part animated adventure celebrates Sonic Colors Ultimate. Sonic and Tails travel through Dr. Eggman's amusement park to rescue captured Wisps and stop his latest plans.

Sonic the Hedgehog 2

Year: 2022

Type: Live-Action/CGI Movie

Sonic returns in a sequel that introduces Tails and Knuckles. Dr. Robotnik searches for the powerful Master Emerald, leading Sonic and his new allies into an exciting adventure to protect the world.

Sonic: Drone Home

Year: 2022

Type: CGI Animated Short Film

Sonic: Drone Home is a short adventure featuring Sonic, Tails, and Knuckles. The trio encounters one of Dr. Robotnik's abandoned drones, leading to a humorous confrontation with the unusual machine.

Sonic Frontiers Prologue: Divergence

Year: 2022

Type: Animated Prologue Short

This animated short focuses on Knuckles the Echidna before the events of Sonic Frontiers. It explores his responsibility as guardian of the Master Emerald and introduces mysteries connected to the Starfall Islands.

Sonic Superstars: Trio of Trouble

Year: 2023

Type: Animated Prologue Short

Trio of Trouble is a traditionally styled animated short connected to Sonic Superstars. It follows Fang the Hunter, Trip, and Dr. Eggman through an adventure that introduces their roles in the game.

★ Sonic on Screen (2024) ★

Knuckles

Year: 2024

Type: Live-Action/CGI Miniseries

Knuckles stars in his own television adventure set within the Sonic movie universe. He begins training Wade Whipple in the ways of an Echidna warrior, leading to an unusual journey filled with action and comedy.

Sonic X Shadow Generations: Dark Beginnings

Year: 2024

Type: Animated Web Miniseries

Dark Beginnings is a three-part animated story exploring Shadow the Hedgehog's past. The series revisits his memories aboard the Space Colony ARK, his relationship with Maria, and the events that shaped his identity.

A Very Sonic Christmas

Year: 2024

Type: Stop-Motion Holiday Short

A Very Sonic Christmas is a stop-motion holiday adventure featuring Team Sonic. After Santa Claus is injured, Sonic and his friends work together to save Christmas.

Sonic the Hedgehog 3

Year: 2024

Type: Live-Action/CGI Movie

Sonic, Tails, and Knuckles return for another cinematic adventure. The powerful Shadow the Hedgehog emerges as a dangerous opponent, forcing Team Sonic into an unexpected alliance with Dr. Robotnik.

★ Recent Sonic Productions (2025–2026) ★

Sonic Presents: Chao Tales

Year: 2025

Type: Animated Social Media Shorts

Chao Tales is a series of short animated adventures following the daily lives of Chao. The episodes feature playful situations, humorous interactions, and the charming personalities of these small creatures.

Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds - The Animation

Year: 2025

Type: Promotional Animated Short

This animated short celebrates the world of Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds. It features Sonic and other familiar characters competing in exciting races across imaginative environments.

Sonic the Hedgehog Presents: The Chaotix Casefiles

Year: 2026

Type: Narrative Audio Mystery Series

The Chaotix Casefiles follows Vector, Espio, and Charmy as they investigate unusual mysteries and complicated cases. This audio adventure focuses on the Chaotix Detective Agency and their teamwork while solving crimes.
