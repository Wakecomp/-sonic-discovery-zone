Welcome to the Zone Explorer!

Explore the many worlds of Sonic the Hedgehog! From tropical islands and ancient ruins to futuristic cities and mysterious dimensions, the Sonic universe is filled with unforgettable locations.

Discover 53 Sonic locations from 1991 through 2023, organized by their original games.

★ Classic Era (1991–1995) ★

Green Hill Zone

Debut: Sonic the Hedgehog (1991)

Featured In: Sonic Generations (Console & 3DS), Sonic Speed Simulator, Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Green Hill is one of the most recognizable locations in the Sonic universe. Its tropical landscape features rolling green hills, palm trees, sunflowers, checkerboard cliffs, and giant loops. The open pathways make it a perfect place for Sonic's high-speed adventures.

Chemical Plant Zone

Debut: Sonic the Hedgehog 2 (1992)

Featured In: Sonic Generations (Console), Sonic Speed Simulator

Chemical Plant is an enormous industrial facility filled with twisting pipes, moving machinery, and dangerous pink chemicals. Its fast pathways and underwater sections challenge players to keep moving while avoiding hazardous obstacles.

Casino Night Zone

Debut: Sonic the Hedgehog 2 (1992)

Featured In: Sonic Generations (3DS)

Casino Night is a brightly illuminated casino environment filled with colorful lights, giant pinball bumpers, slot machines, and spring-loaded obstacles. Players can bounce around the stage while collecting rings and exploring its many pathways.

Emerald Hill Zone

Debut: Sonic the Hedgehog 2 (1992)

Featured In: Sonic Speed Simulator, Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Emerald Hill is a peaceful tropical location featuring grassy slopes, palm trees, ocean views, and winding pathways. Its corkscrew tracks and layered terrain allow Sonic to maintain his speed while exploring the landscape.

Hill Top Zone

Debut: Sonic the Hedgehog 2 (1992)

Featured In: Sonic Speed Simulator

Hill Top takes place high above the clouds in a mountainous region. Blue pine trees and rocky cliffs create a peaceful appearance, but underground lava and volcanic hazards make the environment dangerous.

Stardust Speedway

Debut: Sonic CD (1993)

Featured In: Sonic Speed Simulator, Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Stardust Speedway is a colorful futuristic highway filled with glowing lights, unusual architecture, and winding routes. Its different time periods change the appearance of the environment, and the stage is famous for Sonic's rivalry with Metal Sonic.

Marble Garden Zone

Debut: Sonic the Hedgehog 3 (1994)

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Marble Garden is an ancient environment filled with stone ruins, decorative pillars, and overgrown vegetation. Crumbling structures, spinning tops, and steep cliffs create challenging vertical pathways.

Sunset Park Zone

Debut: Sonic the Hedgehog Triple Trouble (1994)

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Sunset Park is an industrial railway environment illuminated by a dramatic evening sky. Mechanical obstacles, railway tracks, and a memorable train encounter make this location stand out.

Sky Sanctuary Zone

Debut: Sonic & Knuckles (1994)

Featured In: Sonic Generations (Console), Sonic Speed Simulator

Sky Sanctuary is an ancient temple floating high above the clouds. Stone pillars, floating platforms, and impressive architecture create a mysterious atmosphere while challenging Sonic to navigate dangerous heights.

Mushroom Hill Zone

Debut: Sonic & Knuckles (1994)

Featured In: Sonic Generations (3DS)

Mushroom Hill is a colorful forest filled with enormous mushrooms, dense foliage, and spring-like obstacles. Seasonal changes give the environment different colors and appearances.

Speed Slider

Debut: Knuckles' Chaotix (1995)

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Speed Slider is a colorful amusement-park environment filled with twisting routes, unusual geometric structures, and fast-moving slides. Its design emphasizes momentum and continuous movement.

★ Adventure Era (1998–2005) ★

Speed Highway

Debut: Sonic Adventure (1998)

Featured In: Sonic Generations (Console)

Speed Highway takes place in a towering city skyline at night. Sonic races along highways, avoids traffic, and runs down the sides of enormous skyscrapers.

Emerald Coast

Debut: Sonic Adventure (1998)

Featured In: Sonic Generations (3DS)

Emerald Coast is a beautiful seaside resort featuring sandy beaches, wooden walkways, and clear blue ocean water. It is especially famous for the dramatic killer whale chase.

City Escape

Debut: Sonic Adventure 2 (2001)

Featured In: Sonic Generations (Console), Sonic Speed Simulator

City Escape sends Sonic racing downhill through a busy city inspired by San Francisco. Skateboarding, traffic, steep streets, and a massive pursuing truck make this one of the most memorable stages in the series.

Radical Highway

Debut: Sonic Adventure 2 (2001)

Featured In: Shadow Generations, Sonic Generations (3DS), Sonic Speed Simulator, Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Radical Highway is a nighttime highway and suspension-bridge network associated with Shadow. Glowing red bridge cables, grinding rails, and elevated pathways create a dramatic high-speed environment.

Space Colony ARK

Debut: Sonic Adventure 2 (2001)

Featured In: Shadow Generations, Sonic Speed Simulator, Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

The Space Colony ARK is an enormous research station orbiting Earth. Its metallic corridors, laboratories, and advanced technology play an important role in Shadow's history.

Pumpkin Hill

Debut: Sonic Adventure 2 (2001)

Featured In: Sonic Speed Simulator, Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Pumpkin Hill is a spooky mountain environment filled with floating landmasses, pumpkin decorations, graveyards, and ghostly structures. Its eerie atmosphere makes it one of Knuckles' most memorable treasure-hunting stages.

Seaside Hill

Debut: Sonic Heroes (2003)

Featured In: Sonic Generations (Console), Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Seaside Hill combines ancient white-stone ruins with a brilliant blue ocean. Players travel along coastal pathways, speed through loops, and navigate structures built above the water.

Rail Canyon

Debut: Sonic Heroes (2003)

Featured In: Shadow Generations

Rail Canyon is an enormous network of railway tracks suspended over a deep rocky canyon. Players grind between rails and avoid trains while traveling across dangerous gaps.

Casino Park

Debut: Sonic Heroes (2003)

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Casino Park is a colorful casino-themed environment featuring giant dice, playing cards, pinball tables, and glowing decorations. Its unusual obstacles turn the entire stage into an interactive game.

Digital Circuit

Debut: Shadow the Hedgehog (2005)

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Digital Circuit takes place inside a virtual computer network. Glowing data streams, electronic pathways, and abstract digital structures create a futuristic cyberspace environment.

Water Palace

Debut: Sonic Rush (2005)

Featured In: Sonic Generations (3DS), Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Water Palace features elegant architecture surrounded by flowing water. Its fountains, slides, submerged passages, and colorful structures combine beauty with challenging platforming.

★ Modern Era (2006–2010) ★

White Cave

Debut: Sonic Riders (2006)

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

White Cave is an underground racing environment filled with pale rock formations, unusual vegetation, and ancient-looking natural structures. Its winding routes create opportunities for high-speed Extreme Gear racing.

Metal City

Debut: Sonic Riders (2006)

Featured In: Sonic Speed Simulator

Metal City is a futuristic urban racing course filled with elevated highways, neon lighting, mechanical structures, and high-speed routes designed for Extreme Gear competition.

Crisis City

Debut: Sonic the Hedgehog (2006)

Featured In: Sonic Generations (Console)

Crisis City is a ruined future metropolis surrounded by fire and destruction. Collapsed skyscrapers, lava, and violent storms make it one of Sonic's most dangerous urban environments.

Kingdom Valley

Debut: Sonic the Hedgehog (2006)

Featured In: Shadow Generations

Kingdom Valley is a majestic landscape of ancient castles, waterfalls, mountains, and ruined stone pathways. Its dramatic scenery includes high-altitude sections and giant eagles.

Kraken Bay

Debut: Sonic and the Secret Rings (2007)

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Kraken Bay is a pirate-themed coastal location featuring wooden ships, stormy waters, maritime structures, and dangerous obstacles inspired by the world of Arabian Nights.

Dino Jungle

Debut: Sonic and the Secret Rings (2007)

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Dino Jungle is a prehistoric wilderness filled with towering vegetation, ancient creatures, enormous trees, and winding pathways through a dense jungle.

Rooftop Run

Debut: Sonic Unleashed (2008)

Featured In: Sonic Generations (Console), Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Rooftop Run takes place in a colorful European-inspired city. Sonic races across orange rooftops, narrow streets, clock towers, and steep hills in a lively urban environment.

Cool Edge

Debut: Sonic Unleashed (2008)

Featured In: Sonic Speed Simulator

Cool Edge is a frozen landscape filled with snow-covered cliffs, icy passages, and dangerous drops. The stage combines slippery surfaces with high-speed movement through an arctic environment.

Holoska

Debut: Sonic Unleashed (2008)

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Holoska is a snowy region known for its glaciers, frozen waters, and icy settlements. Its harsh winter environment provides the setting for several challenges in Sonic Unleashed.

Dragon Road

Debut: Sonic Unleashed (2008)

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Dragon Road is a mountainous stage inspired by East Asian architecture. Its stone pathways, traditional buildings, and elevated routes create a dramatic high-speed adventure.

Apotos

Debut: Sonic Unleashed (2008)

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Apotos is a peaceful seaside village inspired by Mediterranean coastal communities. White buildings, narrow streets, and beautiful ocean views give the region a welcoming atmosphere.

Misty Lake

Debut: Sonic and the Black Knight (2009)

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Misty Lake is a mysterious lakeside location inspired by the legends of King Arthur. Thick fog covers the water, while wooden docks, ancient stone structures, and medieval scenery create an eerie atmosphere. Its peaceful appearance hides the dangers that Sonic encounters while exploring this magical world.

★ Sonic Colors Era — 2010 ★

Planet Wisp

Year: 2010

Debut Game: Sonic Colors

Featured In: Sonic Generations (Console)

Planet Wisp is a beautiful alien world covered in grassy fields, colorful plants, and unusual natural landscapes. Dr. Eggman has begun transforming parts of the planet into an industrial environment filled with machinery. Sonic must travel through the planet and help protect the Wisps from Eggman's plans.

Tropical Resort

Year: 2010

Debut Game: Sonic Colors

Featured In: Sonic Generations (3DS)

Tropical Resort is the entrance to Dr. Eggman's enormous amusement park in outer space. Bright neon lights, futuristic buildings, colorful attractions, and impressive views of space give the resort an exciting atmosphere. Sonic races through the park while discovering the secrets behind Eggman's attractions.

Starlight Carnival

Year: 2010

Debut Game: Sonic Colors

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Starlight Carnival is a spectacular space environment filled with enormous illuminated ships and glowing pathways. Colorful lights shine against the darkness of outer space. Sonic travels across energy roads and futuristic structures while exploring this incredible interstellar parade.

Sweet Mountain

Year: 2010

Debut Game: Sonic Colors

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Sweet Mountain is a colorful amusement park attraction designed around candy and desserts. Giant cakes, frosting-covered structures, candy decorations, and unusual obstacles fill the landscape. Its playful appearance makes it one of the most imaginative environments in Sonic Colors.

★ Sonic Lost World Era — 2013 ★

Hidden World

Year: 2013

Debut Game: Sonic Lost World

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Hidden World is a strange and colorful location filled with floating platforms, unusual geometric structures, and creative obstacles. Its abstract design creates an unpredictable environment where Sonic must carefully navigate the changing terrain.

Lost Hex

Year: 2013

Debut Game: Sonic Lost World

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

The Lost Hex is a mysterious floating world containing grassy hills, mountains, forests, and ancient ruins. Its unusual landscapes feature curved surfaces and cylindrical pathways that allow Sonic to run in different directions. The Lost Hex is also home to the dangerous Deadly Six.

★ Sonic Mania Era — 2017 ★

Mirage Saloon Zone

Year: 2017

Debut Game: Sonic Mania

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Mirage Saloon is a Wild West-inspired desert filled with sandy canyons, giant cacti, railway tracks, and old-fashioned saloons. Sonic encounters moving trains and unusual mechanical obstacles while exploring the desert. The stage also features an exciting airplane sequence with Tails.

★ Sonic Forces Era — 2017 ★

Sunset Heights

Year: 2017

Debut Game: Sonic Forces

Featured In: Shadow Generations, Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Sunset Heights is a city devastated by Dr. Eggman's invasion. Burning skies, damaged buildings, barricades, and dangerous robots fill the streets. The location reflects the ongoing conflict between Eggman's army and the Resistance.

Metro City

Year: 2017

Debut Game: Sonic Forces

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Metro City is a futuristic urban environment filled with towering skyscrapers, neon lights, highways, and mechanical structures. Elevated pathways and dangerous obstacles create a fast-paced setting inspired by the technological side of Sonic's world.

Lost Valley

Year: 2017

Debut Game: Sonic Forces

Featured In: Sonic Speed Simulator

Lost Valley is a dry desert landscape filled with rocky cliffs, sandy terrain, and damaged structures. Dangerous machines patrol the area, creating obstacles throughout Sonic's journey. Its harsh environment reflects the destruction caused by Eggman's forces.

Mystic Jungle

Year: 2017

Debut Game: Sonic Forces

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Mystic Jungle is a mysterious rainforest filled with enormous trees, glowing crystals, dense vegetation, and winding pathways. The forest contains unusual natural structures and dangerous obstacles that make exploration challenging.

★ Team Sonic Racing Era — 2019 ★

Sky Road

Year: 2019

Debut Game: Team Sonic Racing

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Sky Road is a high-altitude racing course surrounded by beautiful clouds and open skies. Its floating tracks, sharp turns, and speed-boosting sections create exciting racing challenges far above the ground.

★ Sonic Frontiers Era — 2022 ★

Chaos Island

Year: 2022

Debut Game: Sonic Frontiers

Featured In: Shadow Generations

Chaos Island is a volcanic region of the Starfall Islands. Dark mountains, lava, rocky cliffs, and mysterious ancient ruins cover the landscape. Sonic explores the island while discovering more about the ancient civilization that once lived there.

Kronos Island

Year: 2022

Debut Game: Sonic Frontiers

Featured In: Sonic Speed Simulator, Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Kronos Island is a large natural environment filled with grassy hills, forests, waterfalls, and ancient stone ruins. Its open-zone design allows Sonic to freely explore the landscape, discover hidden challenges, and investigate the island's mysterious history.

Cyber Space

Year: 2022

Debut Game: Sonic Frontiers

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Cyber Space is a mysterious digital dimension created from memories and stored information. It contains virtual recreations of familiar Sonic environments, glowing structures, and futuristic pathways. Sonic travels through these digital stages to complete high-speed challenges.

★ Sonic Superstars Era — 2023 ★

Speed Jungle

Year: 2023

Debut Game: Sonic Superstars

Featured In: Sonic Speed Simulator

Speed Jungle is a tropical rainforest filled with giant trees, thick vines, glowing plants, and winding pathways. Sonic travels through the dense jungle while avoiding obstacles and using the natural environment to maintain his speed.

Cyber Station

Year: 2023

Debut Game: Sonic Superstars

Featured In: Sonic Speed Simulator

Cyber Station is a colorful digital world filled with geometric platforms, glowing electronic structures, and futuristic obstacles. The stage features unusual transformations and creative gameplay mechanics that make it different from traditional Sonic environments.

Sky Temple

Year: 2023

Debut Game: Sonic Superstars

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Sky Temple is an ancient sanctuary floating high above the clouds. Stone platforms, ruined structures, and powerful winds create a dangerous aerial environment. Sonic must travel across elevated pathways while avoiding hazards.

Bridge Island

Year: 2023

Debut Game: Sonic Superstars

Featured In: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

Bridge Island is a beautiful tropical environment featuring sandy beaches, palm trees, rocky cliffs, and clear blue ocean water. Its grassy pathways, loops, and coastal scenery make it an exciting introduction to the world of Sonic Superstars.
