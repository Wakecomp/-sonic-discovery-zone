Welcome to the Sonic Games Encyclopedia!

Explore the history of Sonic the Hedgehog video games! From the original Sega Genesis adventures to modern games, Sonic has traveled through many exciting worlds.

Discover 24 Sonic games released between 1991 and 2023, including their stories, gameplay features, and memorable innovations.

★ Classic Era (1991–1995) ★

Sonic the Hedgehog

Release Year: 1991

Sonic the Hedgehog introduced the world to Sega's famous blue hero. Players race through colorful environments, collect rings, and rescue animals captured by Dr. Robotnik. The game established Sonic's signature speed-based platforming.

Sonic the Hedgehog 2

Release Year: 1992

Sonic returns for another high-speed adventure, joined by his new friend Miles "Tails" Prower. The game introduces the Spin Dash, larger stages, and exciting new challenges as Sonic attempts to stop Dr. Robotnik's Death Egg plans.

Sonic CD

Release Year: 1993

Sonic CD takes Sonic on a time-traveling adventure where players explore different versions of the same environments. The game introduces Amy Rose and Metal Sonic while featuring unique past, present, and future mechanics.

Sonic the Hedgehog 3

Release Year: 1994

Sonic and Tails continue their adventure on Angel Island, where they encounter Knuckles the Echidna. The game introduces elemental shields, new abilities, and a story that unfolds throughout its interconnected stages.

Sonic & Knuckles

Release Year: 1994

Sonic & Knuckles continues the Angel Island adventure and allows players to control Knuckles. Its famous lock-on technology lets players connect compatible cartridges, including Sonic the Hedgehog 3, to expand the experience.

Sonic Triple Trouble

Release Year: 1994

Released for the Sega Game Gear, Sonic Triple Trouble follows Sonic and Tails as they search for the Chaos Emeralds. Along the way, they encounter familiar enemies and the treasure hunter Nack the Weasel.

Knuckles' Chaotix

Release Year: 1995

Knuckles' Chaotix is a Sega 32X adventure featuring Knuckles and several new characters. Its unusual partner system connects two characters with an elastic tether, creating unique movement and teamwork mechanics.

★ Adventure Era (1998–2005) ★

Sonic Adventure

Release Year: 1998

Sonic Adventure brought Sonic into a fully three-dimensional world. Players experience six different character stories, explore adventure fields, and discover the mystery surrounding Chaos and the Master Emerald.

Sonic Adventure 2

Release Year: 2001

Sonic Adventure 2 features two connected story campaigns: Hero and Dark. The game introduces Shadow the Hedgehog and Rouge the Bat, while combining high-speed action, treasure hunting, and shooting stages.

Sonic Heroes

Release Year: 2003

Sonic Heroes introduces a team-based gameplay system where players control three characters together. Each team uses Speed, Power, and Flight abilities to overcome obstacles and defeat enemies throughout colorful stages.

Shadow the Hedgehog

Release Year: 2005

Shadow takes the leading role in this darker Sonic spin-off. Players explore his mysterious past through branching missions and different story paths. The game introduces weapon-based combat and choices that influence Shadow's journey.

Sonic Rush

Release Year: 2005

Sonic Rush brings fast-paced side-scrolling action to the Nintendo DS. It introduces Blaze the Cat and the Tension Gauge, allowing players to perform powerful speed boosts while racing through stages.

★ Modern Era (2006–2010) ★

Sonic Riders

Release Year: 2006

Sonic Riders introduces high-speed racing on futuristic hoverboards known as Extreme Gear. Players compete across unusual tracks while using air currents, shortcuts, and special abilities. The game also introduces the Babylon Rogues.

Sonic the Hedgehog (2006)

Release Year: 2006

Sonic the Hedgehog features an ambitious story involving Sonic, Shadow, and Silver the Hedgehog. Players explore different campaigns while uncovering the mystery of Solaris and the kingdom of Soleanna.

Sonic and the Secret Rings

Release Year: 2007

Sonic enters the magical world of Arabian Nights in this Storybook adventure. The game uses motion controls and automatically moving forward gameplay as Sonic travels through legendary locations and encounters mysterious characters.

Sonic Unleashed

Release Year: 2008

Sonic Unleashed sends Sonic on a worldwide adventure to restore a shattered planet. Daytime stages focus on high-speed boosting, while nighttime stages transform Sonic into the Werehog, introducing combat and exploration mechanics.

Sonic and the Black Knight

Release Year: 2009

Sonic travels into the world of King Arthur in the second Storybook adventure. Armed with a talking sword named Caliburn, Sonic battles enemies and explores medieval locations inspired by Arthurian legends.

Sonic Colors

Release Year: 2010

Sonic Colors takes place inside Dr. Eggman's enormous amusement park in outer space. Sonic discovers the Wisps, colorful alien creatures that grant special powers. The game combines traditional platforming with high-speed action and creative abilities.

★ Recent Era (2013–2023) ★

Sonic Lost World

Release Year: 2013

Sonic Lost World introduces unusual environments featuring curved terrain, cylindrical pathways, and gravity-based movement. Sonic faces the Deadly Six while using parkour-inspired abilities to explore the Lost Hex.

Sonic Mania

Release Year: 2017

Sonic Mania celebrates the classic Sonic games with detailed pixel art and traditional side-scrolling gameplay. It combines reimagined familiar zones with completely new stages and challenges.

Sonic Forces

Release Year: 2017

Sonic Forces takes place during Dr. Eggman's conquest of the world. Players control Modern Sonic, Classic Sonic, and a custom Avatar character while helping the Resistance fight against Eggman's army.

Team Sonic Racing

Release Year: 2019

Team Sonic Racing focuses on cooperative racing. Players work together in teams of three, share items, use drafting techniques, and perform powerful Team Ultimate boosts to win races.

Sonic Frontiers

Release Year: 2022

Sonic Frontiers introduces open-zone exploration across the mysterious Starfall Islands. Players discover ancient ruins, solve puzzles, battle powerful enemies, and face enormous Titans while uncovering the islands' forgotten history.

Sonic Superstars

Release Year: 2023

Sonic Superstars returns to classic side-scrolling gameplay with modern three-dimensional graphics. Players can control Sonic, Tails, Knuckles, and Amy, explore the Northstar Islands, and use new Chaos Emerald powers. The game also supports local multiplayer for up to four players.
